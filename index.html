Our Services

Comprehensive diagnostic imaging services using the latest technology

MRI Scans

High-resolution magnetic resonance imaging for detailed soft tissue visualization with our 3T MRI scanner.

  • 3 Tesla high-field MRI
  • Short bore design for comfort
  • Advanced sequences available
  • Same-day reporting

CT Scans

Fast, accurate computed tomography imaging with low radiation dose technology for comprehensive diagnosis.

  • 128-slice CT scanner
  • Low-dose protocols
  • Cardiac CT available
  • 3D reconstruction

X-Ray

Digital radiography providing instant, high-quality images with minimal radiation exposure.

  • Digital X-ray systems
  • Immediate image availability
  • Reduced radiation exposure
  • Portable options available

Ultrasound

Non-invasive ultrasound imaging for real-time visualization of organs and blood flow assessment.

  • 3D/4D ultrasound capability
  • Doppler studies
  • Musculoskeletal imaging
  • Pregnancy scanning
